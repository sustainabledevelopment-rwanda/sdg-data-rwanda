computation_definitions: The Consumer Price Index for food and non-alcoholic beverages is based on the price of a household basket of relevant goods relative to a reference period (CPI=100). Please see the linked data source for more information on CPI methodology.
computation_calculations: The Consumer Price Index (CPI) uses a Modified Laspeyres formula to calculate the index. The figures are provided on a monthly basis, with the reference month being February 2014. The figures provided here are annual averages from January to December of each year, calculated as the arithmetic mean of the monthly CPI for the 12 relevant months. Please see the linked data source for more information on the calculation of the CPI. 
comments_limitations: The National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR) publishes the monthly Consumer Price Index by category but does not provide annual averages, which have been calculated specifically for this indicator. indicator_definition: The indicator of food price anomalies (IFPA) identifies market prices that are abnormally high. The IFPA relies on a weighted compound growth rate that accounts for both within year and across year price growth. The indicator directly evaluates growth in prices over a particular month over many years, taking into account seasonality in agricultural markets and inflation, allowing to answer the question of whether or not a change in price is abnormal for any particular period.
