national_indicator_available: Percentage of households using rubbish collection service or publicly managed refuse area
national_indicator_description: This indicator measures the percentage of households whose waste is collected and managed in controlled facilities, that is, whose main method of rubbish disposal is either a rubbish collection service or a publicly managed refuse area.
national_geographical_coverage: Rwanda
computation_units: Percentage (%)
computation_definitions: Households whose waste is collected and managed in controlled facilities are defined as households whose main method of rubbish collection is either a rubbish collection service or a publicly managed refuse area. This excludes households whose main method of rubbish disposal is a compost heap; throwing rubbish in bushes or fields; dumping rubbish in ditches, rivers, or lakes; burning; or other methods.
computation_calculations: (Households using rubbish collection service + Households using publicly managed refuse area) / Total households * 100
other_info: District-level breakdowns are available for this indicator.
comments_limitations: The unit of analysis is the household rather than units of waste. This indicator concerns the main method of rubbish disposal of households; any additional methods used by the same household are not included. Frequency, regularity and proximity of collection points are also not considered. Additionally, reflecting the evolving best practice in poverty measurement, a number of important methodological changes were made for EICV7 (2023-2024). Please see the linked data source for more information on methodology.
