**Non-statistical indicator**

Between 2001 and 2023, Rwanda has consistently notified that export subsidies were not granted to agricultural products. 

Notifications by WTO Members indicating the absence of use of export subsidies are not included in the indicator series. Therefore, no statistical indicator is provided for Rwanda.
